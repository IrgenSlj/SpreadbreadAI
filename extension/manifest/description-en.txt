SpreadbreadAI Review brings human-in-the-loop AI review to LibreOffice Calc.

It connects to a local SpreadbreadAI daemon (default: http://127.0.0.1:8765)
and shows AI-drafted proposals as approvable diffs in a sidebar. The model
can read your workbook and stage proposals — it cannot write to your
spreadsheet without your approval.

Default LLM: Gemma 4 E2B via Ollama, fully offline.
